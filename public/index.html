    Import work in process from CCC ONE

    1. In CCC ONE, open the job list or report you use to track work in process. It needs the RO or workfile number and the body and refinish labor hours; drop-off and target delivery dates make the schedule much better.
    2. Export it to Excel or CSV, or select the rows and copy them.
    3. Paste below (include the header row) or choose the file. Columns are matched automatically; fix any that are wrong. Your matches are remembered for next time.
    4. Each morning after that: export, paste, and click Daily refresh.
